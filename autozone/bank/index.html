1Have these handy

Grab these before you start, or you’ll get halfway and have to begin again.

  • Your internet banking login The username (or customer number) and password you use on your bank’s website or app. Not your card PIN, and not your fingerprint or Face ID.
  • Your mobile, unlocked and next to you Most banks text you a one-time code partway through. If your phone is flat or in the car, you’ll be stuck.
  • Two spare minutes Do it in one sitting. If you wander off, the page can time out and you’ll start over.
  • The account your pay goes into That’s the one that matters. If your wage lands in one bank and your bills come out of another, do both — run the link once per bank.

3What you’ll see, screen by screen

Six screens, that’s the lot. Here’s each one before you get there, so nothing catches you out.

1

It should say “You’ve been referred by The Buyer Assist Group”. Tick the consent box, then Start.

If it doesn’t say that name, you’re on the wrong link. Come back here and tap the button again.

2

Find your bank

Type the first few letters — “comm”, “anz”, “nab” — and tap your bank’s tile.

Pick the right one. Some banks are listed twice, e.g. personal versus business. Choose the one you log into for your everyday account.

3

Log in as you normally would

Same username and password you use for internet banking. Nobody at Autozone or Buyer Assist ever sees them.

This trips people up: it’s your internet banking password — not your 4-digit card PIN, and not Face ID.

4

Type in the text-message code

Your bank texts you a code, the same as when you pay a new person. Type it in and keep going.

No code arrived? Wait a full minute, then use the “resend” option. Codes expire fast, so use the newest one.

5

Tick your accounts

Tick the everyday account your pay goes into. If a savings or offset account is where your bills come out, tick that too. Then press submit.

When in doubt, tick them all. Extra accounts never hurt you — a missing one means we have to ask you again.

6

Green tick — you’re finished

That’s it. Your statements come straight to us. Nothing to email, nothing to print, nothing to send.

Didn’t reach a green tick? It didn’t go through. Start again from the top, or call Josh.

4When it doesn’t work

Nine times out of ten it’s one of these. Tap to open.

I don’t know my internet banking password

Very common — if you only ever use fingerprint or Face ID, you’ve probably never typed it.

Open your banking app and reset it (usually “Forgot password” on the login screen), or ring your bank. Once you can log into your bank’s own website with it, come back and tap the link again.

It says my login details are wrong

Check three things:

1. You’re using your customer number or username, not your card number.
2. You’re using your internet banking password, not your card PIN.
3. Caps lock is off and there’s no space at the end — phone keyboards love adding one.

Still stuck? Log into your bank’s own website first to prove the details work, then come back.

My bank isn’t in the list

Search by the short name (“ing”, “bendigo”, “greater”) rather than the full legal name.

If it genuinely isn’t there, ring Josh on 0480 852 530 and we’ll do it the manual way instead.

I bank with two different banks

Do the whole thing twice — once per bank. Tap the same link again when you’ve finished the first one.

The one we really need is wherever your wage gets paid in. If your bills come out of the other one, we need that too.

It’s a joint account with my partner

That’s fine and very normal. Send it as you normally log in. You’re only sharing statements, not giving anyone access.

I think I ticked the wrong accounts

No harm done. Just tap the link and run through it again with the right ones — we use the most recent submission.

Nothing loads, or the page keeps spinning

Some banks are slow at busy times — give it a full minute before you give up.

If it’s still stuck: close the tab, turn your phone’s Wi-Fi off and on (or swap to mobile data), and start again. Chrome and Safari work best.

Can I just email you PDFs instead?

You can, as a last resort — email them to josh@thebuyerassist.com.au.

Be warned it’s much slower. They have to be the official PDFs downloaded from your bank — screenshots, photos of a screen and printed pages get knocked back by the lender, and then we’re asking you for them all over again. The link above takes two minutes and is done properly the first time.

“Is this actually safe?”

Fair question. Here’s exactly what happens.

  • It’s read-only. The service can look at your statements and nothing else. No money can be moved, no payments made, no details changed.
  • Nobody here sees your password. Not Autozone, not Buyer Assist, not Josh. You type it into illion’s secure page, never to us.
  • Your login is never stored. It’s encrypted with bank-level 256-bit encryption and discarded the moment the connection is made.
  • Your data stays in Australia, on Australian servers, with the security audited every year by outside IT security firms.
  • It costs you nothing. The service is free for you — the business receiving the statements pays for it.
  • We only get statements. That’s all we ask for and all we’re sent — the same pages you’d have printed out and handed over.

The service is BankStatements.com.au, run by illion Open Data Solutions Pty Ltd (ABN 89 166 277 845) — the same system used by most Australian banks, brokers and lenders. It isn’t provided by your bank, and it doesn’t make any lending decision. Worth knowing: your bank’s terms may limit your rights over unauthorised transactions when you share your internet banking details with a third party. You can read illion’s terms, privacy policy and security page, or ring illion direct on 13 23 33 (press 2, then 2), 7am–7pm weekdays AEST.
