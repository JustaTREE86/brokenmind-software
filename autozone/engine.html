Google review link

Google Business Profile → Ask for reviews → copy link. It looks like g.page/r/XXXXXXXX/review and opens the star box straight away. Until you paste it, the QR uses a Maps search for the dealership.

Wording

Size

In the print dialog set Paper size to match, Margins: None, and tick Background graphics, then Save as PDF and send that to the printer. The SVG button gives you the bare QR as vector art — razor sharp at any size — if the print shop would rather drop it into their own layout. If the file does not save (that happens when you open this from a shared link rather than the copy on your computer), the code is copied to your clipboard: paste it into Notepad and save it as review-qr.svg.

Finance defaults

Leaving the rate off is the safer default — once you advertise a rate to consumers you generally have to show a comparison rate beside it. Worth a quick check with your aggregator before you turn this on.

Wording, branding & print

The warranty strip shows whatever each car is advertised with on the website. Blank the warranty field on a card to drop the strip from that card only.

Settings and any vehicles you add are stored in this browser only. Use “Copy my stock as text” to move them to another computer.
