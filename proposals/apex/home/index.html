Questions

The ten we get asked every week.

Marked up as structured data, so these answers can appear directly in Google search results.

What documents are required?

Most lenders only need two recent consecutive payslips and your driver licence to get started. Some loan types need more, and we tell you exactly what before you send anything.

How long does approval take?

Many applications are approved same day, with some lenders coming back within a few hours. More complex files take longer, and we keep you updated at every step.

Will applying affect my credit score?

We review your situation upfront, before any credit check happens. That means we only lodge with a lender that fits, and your file does not wear a string of unnecessary enquiries.

Can you help if I have been declined elsewhere?

Often, yes. The panel includes lenders who look past standard bank criteria. We assess the situation honestly and tell you what the path forward looks like.

Do you charge any fees?

There is a fee, and it is not charged upfront. It is included in the loan and paid at settlement, so you are not out of pocket to apply. We show you the figure before you sign anything.

Can I pay the loan out early?

Many lenders allow early payout with minimal or no break fees. If that flexibility matters to you, say so at the start and we will weight the panel toward it.
