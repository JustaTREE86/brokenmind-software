01

Built on the GoDaddy Website Builder

The page identifies itself as Go Daddy Website Builder 8.0. You do not own the code, you cannot add a calculator, an application flow or a partner login, and every page you ever publish inherits the same 2.5 MB shell. It is a ceiling, and you have already hit it.

Critical
02

No licence or entity disclosure anywhere on the site

No ACL, no credit representative number, no ABN, no privacy policy, no credit guide. /privacy, /privacy-policy and /credit-guide all return 404. For a credit assistance business this is the first thing a compliance reviewer, a lender BDM or a switched-on referral partner looks for.

Critical
03

"Apply Now" is a contact form, not an application

Name, email, phone, a free text box and a file attachment. No loan type logic, no asset detail, no employment or income capture, no privacy consent tick, no routing into a CRM. Every enquiry still gets rebuilt over the phone from scratch.

Critical
04

Invisible to search

Zero JSON-LD on any page, so Google has no idea you are a finance broker in Eight Mile Plains. No canonical tags. Page titles read "Apply Now" and "Consumer Finance" with no brand and no location. Your FAQ content is exactly what a FAQ rich result is built from, and it is not marked up.

High
05

No repayment calculator

The single highest intent tool on any finance broker site, and the most linked to page on ours. Someone pricing a $45,000 caravan wants a weekly number before they hand over a phone number. Right now they get that number on a competitor's site.

High
06

Four Instagram embeds carrying most of the page weight

Unoptimised social images, four of them over 250 KB each, loaded on the home page before anything a visitor came for. Social proof is right, this delivery of it is not.

High
07

The Financial Education page is a placeholder

"Financial Education Series, Coming 2026" with no articles behind it. It sits in your main navigation. That page should be the engine that brings strangers to the site, and instead it is the page that tells them you have not started.

High
08

Small trust leaks

Footer copyright still reads 2025. A "Powered by GoDaddy" badge sits under your brand. The same hero image is used as the social preview for every page, so every link you post to Facebook looks identical.

Medium
09

No measurement worth the name

A Meta pixel fires, and that is it. No conversion events, no call tracking, no idea which of your four channels actually produces an enquiry. You are posting into the dark.

Medium

On the compliance point

We are flagging what is not visible on the site, not making a call about your licensing. You may well hold everything through your licensee. The issue is that a visitor, a lender and ASIC all read the website, and the website currently says nothing.

Part of this build is a Privacy Policy and Credit Guide page plus a disclosure footer carrying your ACL or credit representative number, ABN, AFCA membership and complaints process. We draft it, your licensee signs off on the wording before it goes live.
